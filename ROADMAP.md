# Field Kit: from pilots to standalone products

| Product | Everyday promise | Next build | Paid reason to stay | Pilot test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LastSlot | Recover revenue from appointments that would otherwise go empty. | Give a business one persistent page for all open slots. Let customers opt into a waitlist by service, provider and time. Notify the matches and stop offering the slot after it is claimed. | A business can see recovered bookings and revenue; add calendar sync after the manual pilot works. | Pilot with one hair or nail studio. Compare recovered bookings, time to fill, and manager effort across ten cancellations. |
| BorrowBoard | Find equipment, see who has it, and get it back on time. | Add advance reservations, due reminders, and a return and condition history. | Teams pay for a coordinator view with multiple locations, staff access, and exports. | Track 20 items with one club, school, or makerspace. Compare time spent chasing returns. |
| RainPlan | Publish one reliable update when a plan changes. | Give organizers a season or program page with linked events. Let followers opt into alerts for their own group. | Clubs and programs pay for branded pages, alert delivery, and several staff editors. | Run a 4-week program and compare duplicate messages and missed changes before and after. |

LastSlot is the first revenue test: an otherwise empty appointment makes the result measurable. Start with manual posts so we can see whether matching alerts and calendar sync would be used before building both.
