Fictional company and records. Changes stay in this session. No emails are sent and no books are changed.

Pilot setup checklist

For a setup conversation, bring the name of your accounting tool and a rough weekly invoice and receipt count. Agree on a secure handoff before sharing any exports.

  • One company and one person to approve the weekly work
  • An open-invoice export and a receipt or expense list
  • A weekly cutoff and an agreed place to deliver the report

There’s no upload or payment step in this sample.
